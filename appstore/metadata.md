## Description (EN)
Tonly is the fastest TON wallet built on the revolutionary W5 smart contract standard.

Send and receive TON instantly with near-zero fees. Manage your Jetton tokens, explore your NFT collection, and stay on top of your portfolio — all in one clean, minimal app.

Key Features:
- W5-powered transactions for lightning speed
- Secure storage with Face ID and passcode protection
- Real-time balance updates
- Full Jetton token support
- NFT gallery with collection view
- .ton domain resolution
- Staking pool overview
- Home screen widget
- Available in English and Russian

Your keys, your crypto. Tonly never stores your private keys on any server. Everything stays on your device, secured by the Keychain.
